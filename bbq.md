# BBQ Sauce

> From [Smitten Kitchen](https://smittenkitchen.com/2006/08/homemade-barbecue-sauce/)

## Ingredients

- Yellow onion | 1 1/2 cups | minced
- Garlic cloves | 3 | minced
- Vegetable oil | 6 tablespoons |
- Tomato paste | 1 cup (265g) |
- Apple cider vinegar | 1 cup (235ml) |
- Honey | 1 cup (335g) |
- Worcestershire sauce | 1/2 cup (120ml) |
- Smooth dijon mustard | 1 cup (240g) |
- Soy sauce | 1/2 cup (120ml) |
- Hoisin sauce | 1 cup (335g) |
- Mild chili powder | 2 tablespoons |
- Ground cumin | 1 tablespoon |
- Red pepper flakes | to taste |

## Directions

In a large saucepan on low heat, cook the onions and garlic in oil for 10 to 15 minutes, until the onions are translucent and soft, but not browned.

Add the rest of the ingredients. Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, for 30 minutes.

Use immediately or store in the fridge for up to a month.
