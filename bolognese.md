# Bolognese

## Ingredients

- Ground beef | 500g |
- Onion | 1 |
- Red pepper | 1 |
- Oregano | 1 tsp |
- Thyme | sprinkle |
- Tinned chopped tomatoes | 400g can |
- Tomato purée | 1 tsp |
- Stock cube | 1 |

## Directions

Fry the beef, onion, and pepper together slowly. Break up the meat as it fries.

Once the meat is browned, add some oregano, a little thyme, the tinned chopped tomatoes, a cup of water, tomato purée, black pepper, and a stock cube (or salt).

Let it bubble for a couple of hours. Make sure it doesn't dry out too much.

Taste and add extra seasoning or herbs until it tastes right!
